👋 Hello, grown-up!

SkillSpring is a small, screen-time-friendly learning prototype for ages roughly 3–7. You can play together and talk about the questions.

  • Each adventure is four short questions with a natural ending.
  • Spoken prompts can be turned on or off with the speaker button.
  • No account, chat, advertising, third-party analytics, or remote AI service.
  • Stars, stickers, and learning progress are stored only in this browser, if available.
  • Question difficulty gently adjusts with practice. This is not a diagnostic assessment.

Tip: For pre-readers, tap the speaker 🔊 after opening a game. iPad browsers may require a tap before audio starts.
